Hammer Camp

Week-long overnight camps that forge boys into capable, confident young men — survival skills and military-style challenges with a combat veteran, jiu-jitsu and wrestling with a purple belt, recovery in the sauna and cold plunge, and real brotherhood around the fire. Ages 13–17, June through July.
