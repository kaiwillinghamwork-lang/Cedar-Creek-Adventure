Explore the

PROPERTY

Twenty-eight acres of creek-side timber built around the days you'll remember. Warm up in the Owner's Lodge sauna, gather for meals at the Central Lodge, sleep in your own cabin in the timber, or roll in and settle a rig at the RV & tent sites. Every corner opens onto East Fork Cedar Creek — and if you'd rather we tailor the whole trip, just say the word.

Run by People Who

LIVE IT

Cedar Creek is guided by Mykle — a combat veteran who's spent his life in the backcountry — alongside Kai, a jiu-jitsu purple belt with years on the mats. Between them they bring the discipline, woodcraft, and know-how that make a trip out here feel effortless.

They handle the hard parts — the meals, the fire, the gear, the game — so your only job is to show up and soak it in.
